# Data notice

`data/paper_interventions.csv` is a processed event-level table derived for reproducibility from the UNICON university utilities dataset described by Moraliyage et al. (2022), *UNICON: An Open Dataset of Electricity, Gas and Water Consumption in a Large Multi-Campus University Setting*, DOI 10.1109/HSI55341.2022.9869498.

The raw UNICON files are not redistributed here. Users should consult the original data source and its applicable terms before reuse. The additional files in `data/examples/` are synthetic demonstration portfolios created for this software and are not empirical findings.
